For each mountain, we find the nearest higher mountain to the left and to the right. Since the mountains are

This problem can be solved with a technique known as centroid decomposition.
The idea is to recursively split the tree into disjoint trees. We start by selecting a node from the tree. Then, we

same procedure for the resulting new trees.

that it is a centroid of the tree, i.e., a node such that when it is appointed the root of the tree, each subtree has at

nodes.

that each node will be part of at most

At each step, a centroid node can be found with a greedy algorithm, which works as follows: root the tree, traverse
recursively from the root, each time choosing a child with a subtree size of at least

the traversal has arrived at a centroid node.

position of dist stores the amount of nodes at distance from the centroid. To ensure that the counted paths
